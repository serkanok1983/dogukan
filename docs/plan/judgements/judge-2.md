# Judge 2 — early-education expert + the father

Lens: does it teach real skills aligned with Turkish okul öncesi / 1. sınıf outcomes, nurture curiosity about basic science, respect a 6-year-old emerging reader, protect him, and give the father insight without surveillance creep? Feasibility is judged against one session of ~8 parallel agents in this codebase (Next 16 static export, 32 canvas games with a thin shared infra, 39 text quizzes on 7 shared components, 14-topic encyclopedia at 3rd–5th-grade register, no tests, no PWA).

I read all four proposals end to end and the five codebase maps, and spot-checked `src/activities/data/quizData.ts` and `src/lib/menu.ts`.

### 1.1 game-designer.md — "Bal Adaları"

**Engagement 9.** This is the only proposal that answers the father's actual sentence: "Süper Ayı ve Minecraft seviyesinde oyunlar sunamadığım için." A pannable island map with a talking bear, visible star fill per island, a 2-D block sandbox with saved worlds and a "Babaya göster" button, a swipeable finite science feed, cosmetics on the companion, and a game-feel pass on the first ten seconds of Süper Ayı. The day-7 story (he opens the app himself because the bear promised a hat; he builds a mirrored castle; the shadow episode sends him outside) is believable for this child. The sample capsule episode `isik-ve-golge` (12 cards, ≤ 8 words, picture answers, "Birlikte bakalım" on wrong picks, mission card with no next button) is the single best piece of concrete child-register content across all four documents.

**Learning 7.** F8 is a real exercise engine: MEB ses grupları in the right order, rows of five, ten-frame to 20, real symmetry/tangram/trace replacing the three fake exercises, `güvenli-hayat` with 112/trafik/"yabancıdan bir şey alma"/ekran molası, a register rule ("≤ 7 words, concrete nouns, no metalanguage"). Blok Dünyası challenges (`say`: exactly 8 blocks forming a connected bridge; `ayna`: mirror the half; `oruntu`: continue ABAB) are genuine 1. sınıf matematik (sayma, simetri, örüntü) in a form a 6-year-old will choose voluntarily. Deductions: the mastery model is thin (level up after 3, down after 2, no spaced review, no prerequisites, no due-skill logic), and the Bal Kapısı sample asks "Hangisi *k* sesiyle başlar?" — Turkish TTS will say "ke", which is precisely the ses esaslı error a 1. sınıf teacher spends September undoing. Rewards (stickers for letter groups) are fine, but nothing here speaks the school's language back to the father the way pedagogy's MEB codes do.

**Feasibility 5.** The Phase-1 table is honest about scope and that is the problem: E8 alone is asked for an SVG icon set, 6 island illustrations, a 6-frame bear atlas with 20 cosmetic overlays, 120 flat SVG stickers, a sound palette extension and a rewrite of every intro — that is weeks, not a session. E4 is asked for a block engine, atlas renderer, 5 validators, 15 seeded challenges, PNG export and a walking bear. E5 for 16 generators × 3 levels plus four rebuilt games. The risk is eight half-built systems. The ideas survive; the Phase-1 cut does not.

**Wellbeing 7.** Deterministic rewards, no timers, no purchases, finite feed, violent games behind the parent gate, "Bal uyuyor" instead of a hard lock, metrics the father tunes on — all good. But: honey is spent per run (−1 even if he dies in 8 s), the gate is explicitly modelled on "watch an ad to continue", the first three days give double honey (hook-then-squeeze), the streak sits on the icon badge (a count badge, and impossible under static export anyway), and "Bal's room" is a Tamagotchi care framing. The sibling wellbeing document catches every one of these; the synthesis must apply its fixes.

### 1.2 pedagogy.md

**Engagement 6.** The daily loop (glowing Günün Görevi, energy tank, streak, sticker slot, due-review quest) is sound, and the Phase-2 idea of letter honey pots inside Süper Ayı where the bear shouts the sound is the best engagement–learning fusion in the set. But the day-1 script opens with three learning tasks before "Oyun Parkı açıldı", and F6 puts a lock icon on the arcade island "until quest done or energy > 0". For a child who already owns Super Bear Adventure, the first impression would be "they took my game and added homework" — exactly the failure mode the wellbeing proposal names. No companion character, no sandbox, no creative surface; the arcade is tolerated rather than designed.

**Learning 10.** This is what a good 1. sınıf teacher would write. A 34-skill graph with MEB codes and prerequisite edges (`tr.ses-ayirt → tr.ses-harf → tr.hece → tr.kelime → tr.cumle`; `mat.sayma(L2) → mat.toplama(L1)`), a mastery store with Leitner boxes so yesterday's letter comes back, scaffold-on-miss (retry with hint → reveal with one spoken sentence → re-queue), interleaving (4 focus + 1 due + 1 adjacent), `TapCount` with the voice counting along (one-to-one correspondence, the way MEB teaches it), rows of five and ten-frames and number lines (the representations his teacher will draw), lowercase dik temel harfler first, words restricted to letters he has mastered so he can actually decode, the honest statement that TTS says letter names not sounds and the rule that the engine never asks TTS for a bare consonant, Hece Treni, dinlediğini anlama as a named gap, Hayat Bilgisi as a third of the curriculum including the "ekranda korkunç bir şey gördün → kapat ve büyüğüne göster" scenario (protection taught, not only enforced), Dur-Git for inhibition (the EF most predictive of early school success), process praise, a 69-slug keep/rebuild/merge/remove table that removes out-of-grade items (alfabe-sirasi and çift-tek are 2. sınıf; para üstü; "Jilet" as the J clue), and success measures the father can read after two weeks. The parent panel speaks in kazanım so he can talk to the teacher.

**Feasibility 7.** The content list is long but the phasing is disciplined (an explicit "not in Phase 1" list). The one oversized Phase-1 item is `TraceCanvas` with 29 + 29 letter stroke paths and 10 digits — hand-authoring 68 point paths is a day of careful work on its own; Phase 1 should ship groups 1–2 only. Six renderers plus 15 generators plus 52 capsules plus the shell is still a lot for its E1–E5, but the dependency on frozen `types.ts` is the right way to parallelise.

**Wellbeing 8.** Energy is parent-set and only refilled by learning; Mola offers a capsule, never more arcade; soft bedtime; weekly streak freeze; "never phrase learning as a cost"; `dovus-arenasi` parent-only; the extrinsic-reward caveat is acknowledged and mitigated. Deductions: the arcade lock behind the quest contradicts its own "never phrase learning as a cost", and energy + stars gives the child two meters.

### 1.3 mobile-production.md

**Engagement 6.** Everything here is the precondition for engagement rather than engagement itself: installed icon, no blank hydration gap, crisp DPR canvases at the same speed on 60/120 Hz, thumb-zone controls, haptics, no accidental exit, offline on the school run, a voiced 2-second star card instead of a rival paragraph. The world map and Devam tile are in scope, but quests/rewards are empty modules and Mola is Phase 2. Day 7 is "the same games, now real" — necessary, not sufficient.

**Learning 3.** Deliberately defers pedagogy; the only learning-adjacent deliverables are `speech.ts`, `SayButton` and a `slug` prop on `QuizGame`. The lise-register quizzes would still be on the child's screen at the end of this plan.

**Feasibility 9.** The most precise document: every Next 16 claim cites a doc path (manifest body not basePath-prefixed, `apple-icon` PNG-only, `headers()` unsupported under export so `updateViaCache: "none"`, `unstable_retry` in 16.2, `global-error` constraints), exact line numbers for the 14 lint errors with structural fixes, a frozen-contract list, an integration order, "done when" criteria per stream, a test suite with concrete assertions, Playwright against the preinstalled browser path. The `{Activity()}` → `<Activity/>` fix is a latent rules-of-hooks bug nobody else noticed. The event-sourced progress store with commutative/idempotent merge is the right persistence design for a child who will use two devices.

**Wellbeing 6.** Zero child-reachable external links, Firebase anonymous auth and rules so a minor's log is not world-readable, no Web Push by design, no install nagging to the child, crash log for the father, error boundaries so a crash does not become "open YouTube instead". But no time budget, no praise model, no breaks, no bedtime in Phase 1 — it treats wellbeing as a later feature, which for this child it is not.

### 1.4 parent-wellbeing.md

**Engagement 7.** It adds no new play content but protects what engages: Süper Ayı stays the hero tile with his old high score voiced ("Rekorun hâlâ burada"), the child names the bear, three grace days, two equal buttons on every sheet, breaks as movement games with the bear counting, a bedtime screen, co-play that unlocks the father's games. The transition plan (F10) is the most important engagement document of the four because it is about the first impression. The unskippable 60-second break will be resented once; the author says so and plans for the first one to be co-played.

**Learning 6.** No content of its own, but three things here are pedagogy: `praise.ts` with process-only lines and a banned-word test (`zeki|akıllı|en iyi|mükemmel|geçtin`); the "Bu akşam birlikte" card (today's mission + one `talkQuestion` + one skill tip like "yemekte 'kkk-edi' diye ilk sesi uzatın") — this is where school transfer actually happens, at the dinner table; and Hareket Kartları that count to ten and do ritmik sayma on the way to resting the eyes.

**Feasibility 8.** Pure rule modules (`wellbeing.ts`, `sessionClock.ts`, `praise.ts`, `breaks.ts`) with tests, overlays mounted once in `layout.tsx`, two engineers. The clock edge cases (background, midnight in `Europe/Istanbul`, clock tampering, two tabs, offline) are thought through. The coordination risk is real: it depends on six sibling contracts.

**Wellbeing 10.** This is the spec: one child-visible meter; limits never cut a run; countdown predicted at −5/−1; LimitScreen with today's stars, tomorrow's preview and a mission; breaks every 20 min at the next natural pause; evening mode, bedtime and `wakeFrom`; 3-day grace; co-play behind an arithmetic gate that does not drain honey; praise rules; the anti-pattern list (no push, no badges, no loot, no streak reset, no autoplay, no infinite feed); zero external surface enforced by a post-build grep; a dashboard that answers "how much, what, is it working, what do we do tonight" in 20 seconds; day buckets and session end-reasons instead of raw timestamps ("surveillance without insight" — the exact line I was looking for as the father). One caution for the synthesis: `bedtimeBlocks` and `sessionsEndedBy` are useful as daily counts; do not render them as a timestamped list of night-time attempts.

## 3. Ideas to REJECT (with reasons)

1. **Honey spent per arcade run (−1 per run, cap 9)** [game-designer F4/F5]. A child who dies in 8 seconds loses a unit and learns that trying is expensive; it also makes the gate fire on skill, not on time. Drain by active arcade time instead (wellbeing F1).
2. **Honey as both the arcade budget and a shop currency** (honey-blocks for lava/elmas blocks) [game-designer F6]. A budget meter that is also money teaches the wrong relationship; cosmetics and block types are bought with stars.
3. **Two child-visible meters (honey + energy)** [game-designer F4 + pedagogy §2.5]. One jar. `progress.honey` is derived from `wellbeing.honeyLeft`, not a second counter.
4. **Arcade island locked until Günün Görevi is done / energy > 0** [pedagogy F6, day-1 script]. This is "önce ders" in a padlock costume and the fastest way to lose him to Super Bear on day 1. The arcade is open from the first minute with the base jar; the quest glows and extends the jar.
5. **Bal Kapısı as "the honest version of watch an ad to continue", sliding over a paused run** [game-designer F5]. An interrupt-to-continue gate is the mechanism of the habit he already has. Keep the surface (3 voiced adaptive items from the weakest skill — that is a good practice placement) but offer it as one of two equal buttons on the RewardSheet when the jar is empty ("🍯 Bal topla" / "🗺️ Harita"), at most twice per session, never as a wall.
6. **Double free honey for the first three days** [game-designer honey table]. Hook-then-squeeze. Replace with the 3-day grace with no jar gating and a one-sentence explanation by the bear on day 3 (wellbeing F10).
7. **Streak sun on the home-screen icon badge** [game-designer day-7 vision]. A count badge is an anti-pattern (wellbeing) and the Badging API is not available to a static-export PWA without push anyway.
8. **"Bal's room" as a character to care for / Tamagotchi hook** [game-designer F10]. Care-and-neglect mechanics produce guilt-driven opens. Keep cosmetics and the sticker album as a plain collection with no neglect state.
9. **Phase-1 art scope: 120 SVG stickers, 20 cosmetic overlays, 6-frame bear atlas, 6 island illustrations, icon set, 24 earcons by one engineer** [game-designer E8/F13]. Infeasible in a session. Phase 1: 7 flat SVG islands, an inline `Icon.tsx`, a 3-frame bear, ~30 sticker ids rendered as emoji-on-card; the rest Phase 2.
10. **Pointer-drag sticker peel reveal** [game-designer sticker table]. Slot-machine-adjacent reveal theatre; auto-reveal after 1 s with a sound is enough.
11. **Web Share of Blok screenshots from the child surface** [game-designer F6]. The OS share sheet is an exit to WhatsApp/Instagram — an external surface by another door. Firebase gallery only; the father shares from `/bilgi`.
12. **58 letter stroke paths + 10 digits in Phase 1** [pedagogy E2]. Hand-authoring 68 stroke-order paths is a day on its own; Phase 1 ships groups 1–2 (12 letters, lower + upper) and digits 0–9; the generator reads what exists.
13. **Mola/time budget deferred to Phase 2** [mobile F17]. For this child the limit layer is a P0 Phase-1 safety feature, not polish; the wellbeing engineer owns it from hour 0.
14. **Rival messages mid-run ("Serkan'ı geçtin! 🏆", "Bu oyunda lider: …")** [current code; all four agree]. Parent-only stat, `rivalVisible` default off. A 6-year-old competing against his father's high score is motivating for a week and discouraging after.
15. **Bare-consonant TTS prompts ("Hangisi *k* sesiyle başlar?")** [game-designer F1/F5 samples]. TTS says "ke"; MEB ses esaslı needs the sound. Pedagogy's rule wins: word + picture, hint re-speaks the word slower; recorded sounds in Phase 2.
16. **`sesli-harf` as "ünlü mü?" yes/no and other metalanguage** [current `quizData.ts`]. Vowel classification by name is metalanguage; merged into Ses Avı at L4 with mouth-shape pictures (pedagogy §4).
17. **Night-time attempt lists / timestamped visit logs in `/bilgi`** [current `activity/dogukan/visits`; wellbeing `bedtimeBlocks` if rendered as a list]. Aggregated counts per day only. Timestamps are surveillance without insight.

## 4. CONFLICTS between proposals (with recommended resolution)

1. **Economy: honey (game-designer) vs energy (pedagogy) vs one jar (wellbeing).** → Wellbeing F1 decides: one honey jar = `arcadeMinutes` as cells (1 🍯 = 2 min), drained by active arcade time only, +2 per completed 6-item set, +1 per capsule episode, +1 per Bal Kapısı (3 items), capped at `arcadeMaxMinutes`; stars buy cosmetics/blocks; Blok free-build and capsules never drain the jar. `progress.honey` is derived.
2. **Day-1 flow: three learning tasks first (pedagogy) vs Süper Ayı hero + name the bear (wellbeing/game-designer).** → Day 1: avatar tap → `NameTheBear` → map with "Devam et ▶ Süper Ayı — Rekorun hâlâ burada" as the hero and a glowing but optional Günün Görevi; 3 grace days; the quest's learning slot is the weakest/due skill from mastery.
3. **Arcade gating: open from minute 1 (game-designer, wellbeing) vs locked until quest (pedagogy).** → Open. Quests are suggestions with a glow; `ChapterEnd` after the quest is the natural stopping point, not the start gate.
4. **Bal Kapısı trigger: sheet over the paused canvas when honey = 0 (game-designer) vs LimitScreen when jar empty (wellbeing).** → Jar empty mid-session → `RewardSheet` shows "🍯 Bal topla (3 soru)" and "🗺️ Harita" as equal buttons; "Bal topla" runs `BalKapisi` from `mastery.pickForGate()` and restarts the game in place with +1 cell; at most twice per session, then only Harita (learning islands and capsules stay open). Daily total cap → `LimitScreen`, no Bal Kapısı.
5. **Companion name: fixed "Bal" (game-designer) vs child-chosen (wellbeing).** → Child picks from four voiced names on first launch; `settings.companionName` defaults to "Bal"; the currency stays "bal" regardless.
6. **Island taxonomy: 6 worlds + hidden Baba ile (game-designer) vs Harf Adası/Şekil Ormanı/Oyun Parkı/Babamla (pedagogy) vs 7 islands derived from `MENU` (mobile).** → Six child islands + hidden Baba ile, names from game-designer (it carries the art brief): Macera Adası, Harf Ormanı, Sayı Dağı, Şekil Köyü (shapes + EF puzzles), Merak Gölü (capsules + HB), Blok Dünyası (shown as "🚧" if it slips); slugs preserved; `worlds.ts` derived from an extended `MENU` as mobile F16 describes.
7. **Exercise/skill types: game-designer `Exercise` (3 levels) vs pedagogy `engine/types.ts` (Visual union, 6 kinds, 5 levels, feedback) vs mobile `progress.skills {level 1|2|3}`.** → Pedagogy's `types.ts` and `SkillState` (superset); 5 levels in the type, L1–L3 content in Phase 1; the progress store's `skills` slice holds pedagogy's `SkillState`.
